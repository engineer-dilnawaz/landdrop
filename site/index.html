GitHub

Share anything with every
device on your Wi-Fi.

Chat and share files of any size between iPhones, Android phones and computers. Nothing goes to the internet: it all stays on your network.

Download for Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and newer). Free. Phones need nothing installed.

How it works

Step 1

Open LANDrop on a Mac

It sits in the menu bar and can start when you log in, so it's there whenever your Mac is awake.

Step 2

Scan the QR code

Any phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi scans it and LANDrop opens in the browser. No app store, no account.

Step 3

Chat and share

Message everyone in the group, or anyone privately. Send photos, videos and files straight across your Wi-Fi.

Made for big files

No size limit

Share a 40 GB movie. Files too big to copy are streamed live from your device: others can watch or save them while you're online.

Private by design

Messages and files travel only over your own network and are kept on your Mac. There's no cloud and no sign-up.

Every device

iPhone, Android, iPad, Windows and Mac: anything with a web browser can join, and sees who else is around.

Help

The Mac says it can't verify LANDrop

LANDrop isn't signed by Apple yet. Open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. If macOS asks whether LANDrop may accept incoming connections, click Allow, or phones can't reach it.

A phone can't open LANDrop

Make sure the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac, not mobile data. On Android Chrome, turn off Settings → Privacy and security → Always use secure connections; LANDrop runs on your local network without https, which that setting blocks.

LANDrop stopped working when my Mac slept

A sleeping Mac can't answer phones. From the menu bar icon, turn on Keep Mac Awake While Plugged In. Closing a laptop's lid still puts it to sleep.
