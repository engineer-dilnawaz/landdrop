Privacy

LANDrop was built so you can share with your own devices without the internet and without worrying about who sees it. Your messages and files travel only over your Wi-Fi and are stored only on your computer. That does not change.

What the app counts

About once a day, while LANDrop is open on your Mac or PC, it sends one small message so its author can see whether it is useful and what to improve. It contains:

  • A random number made on your computer the first time. It is not linked to you, your name, your accounts or your hardware, and it only tells one install from another.
  • The LANDrop version, and whether this is a Mac or Windows PC and which kind of processor.
  • Three totals from the last 24 hours: how many other devices connected, how many messages were sent, and how many files were shared. Only the numbers.
  • Your country, worked out by the website's host from the request. Your IP address is not read or saved.

What it never sends

  • Messages, files, file names or folder names
  • Names of you or your devices, or your network's name or address
  • Anything about your contacts, location or other apps

How it is kept

Counts are stored as daily totals and counters. The random number goes into a structure that keeps only a count and cannot give it back. There is no log of individual messages from your computer.

Turning it off

Open the LANDrop menu and untick Share Anonymous Usage Statistics. Nothing is sent after that. LANDrop works exactly the same either way.

This website

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits (pages viewed, country, device type, and where a visit came from). It sets no cookies and does not follow you from site to site.

Phones and other browsers

Devices that join your LANDrop through a browser send nothing to this website or anyone else. They only talk to your computer on your Wi-Fi.
